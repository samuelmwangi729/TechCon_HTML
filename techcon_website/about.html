Our History

TechCon began in 2010 as a small developer meetup and has since grown into an international technology conference. Over the years, it has evolved into a hub for innovation, collaboration, and inspiration.

Our Mission

At TechCon, we aim to bridge the gap between technology enthusiasts and innovators by providing a platform for learning, networking, and showcasing the future of tech. We believe in open access to knowledge and fostering a global tech community.
